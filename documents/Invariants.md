1. Each doctor (whether general or specialist) can have multiple appointments (visits).
2. The allowed appointment times are from Saturday to Wednesday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
3. The duration of each appointment is between 5 to 15 minutes for a general doctor and between 10 to 30 minutes for a specialist.
4. Each doctor is present at the clinic according to a weekly schedule, and the appointments made with each doctor must be within the time frame of the doctor's presence at the clinic.
5. A patient's appointments should not overlap.
6. A patient should not have more than 2 appointments in one day.
7. A maximum of 2 appointments with a general doctor and 3 appointments with a specialist can overlap.
